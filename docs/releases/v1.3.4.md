# Marvel Champions Digital v1.3.4

Changes since **v1.3.3.1**. In-game version: **1.3.4r**.

- **Jessica Jones and Luke Cage:** Added both complete hero packs, starter decks, obligations, nemesis sets, and player cards, with reviewed artwork for all 81 card faces. Corrected targeting, costs, healing, and card interactions across both packs.
- **Civil War and Synthezoid Smackdown setup:** Choose leaders and schemes separately. Each faction shares five stage-one and five stage-two schemes across both packs, with one scheme allowed per stage.
- **Defaults and faction selection:** Selecting a leader loads its preconstructed schemes and modular sets. New Default buttons restore those choices, while the Resistance/Registration button randomizes modular sets from the matching faction, including Synthezoid Smackdown sets. Required modular sets stay selected.
- **Scheme implementation:** Completed Guerilla Warfare and Superhero Jailbreak, corrected encounter-card searches, and updated scheme artwork. Going Underground, Open Rebellion, and Rallying Call now reveal the leader's side scheme correctly. Fixed Negative Zone Prison completion and Hunting Rebel Heroes damage timing, with expanded coverage across all 20 schemes.
- **Protection Racket:** Corrected triggers and excess damage to match printed cards. Battle in the Bodega now checks attacks on characters in the assigned player's play area.
- **Other card fixes:** Corrected character-specific targeting, unpaid actions, permanent-scheme defeat and flip interactions, Energy Conversion choices, and Drained of Power payments.
- **Artwork and startup:** Fixed hero deck loading and artwork mappings, updated Card Artwork to show **Supported up to: Luke Cage**, and removed the scenario-catalog checksum warning.

Extract into a **new folder**, keeping `marvel-lcg.exe` beside `_internal`. Copy `campaign_settings.json`, personal saves, replays, and custom decks from the previous installation as needed. The existing artwork library is reused. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/sdolle1775/marvel-lcg/blob/v1.3.4/docs/install_guide.md).

The executable matches the [v1.3.4 VirusTotal report](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/file/20d9c8682195c20f52cbd4674d6d118362b178f0973b3552a0f8ceffab871413?nocache=1). Verify the downloaded ZIP with its accompanying `.sha256` file.
